How verification works

VERIFIED: Ticketmaster, an authorized ticketing source, lists the event as on sale or sold out and names this performer. UNCONFIRMED: found, but the performer or sale status could not be confirmed. CANCELLED OR RESCHEDULED: Ticketmaster says so. Ticketmaster does not list every event, so no result is not proof that none exist.
